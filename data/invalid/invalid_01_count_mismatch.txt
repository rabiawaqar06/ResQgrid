# ==========================================================================
# Invalid 01 - Section count does not match the records present
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# PURPOSE
#   The format says a header "KEYWORD count" is followed by EXACTLY count
#   records, and a mismatch must be reported as invalid input.
#
# ERRORS PLANTED
#   E1  LOCATIONS says 6 but only 5 records follow (the next header,
#       CONNECTIONS, appears early).
#   E2  ROBOTS says 2 but 3 records follow (an extra record appears
#       before the REQUESTS header).
#
# EXPECTED RESULT
#   Loading is rejected. Both errors are reported with their line numbers
#   (the loader keeps going after E1 so the user sees every problem in one
#   run). The simulation does not start.
# ==========================================================================

SCENARIO invalid_count_mismatch_01
PARAM max_steps 50
PARAM speed 1
PARAM battery_per_cost_unit 1
PARAM battery_per_work_step 1
PARAM recharge_per_step 10

LOCATIONS 6
1 Command_Center COMMAND
2 Junction_1 JUNCTION
3 Building_1 BUILDING
4 Hospital_1 HOSPITAL
5 Charging_1 CHARGING

CONNECTIONS 5
1 1 2 3 OPEN
2 2 3 4 OPEN
3 2 4 2 OPEN
4 4 5 3 OPEN
5 3 5 6 BLOCKED

ROBOTS 2
1 1 MEDICAL 100 100
2 4 GENERAL 100 100
3 5 FIRE 100 100

REQUESTS 2
1 0 3 MEDICAL 3 2
2 4 4 FIRE 2 2

EVENTS 1
10 BLOCK 2

END
